FAQ

Q: How do I change the time signature?

Tap the green "4/4" badge below the BPM number. A picker will appear with all available time signatures.

Q: Does the metronome work in the background?

Yes. PureBeat continues playing when you lock your screen or switch to another app.

Q: What does Pro include?

Setlist mode, Apple Watch haptic beat, practice statistics, 5 sound presets, and 3 themes. One-time purchase of $2.99 — no subscription.

Q: How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings (gear icon) → "Restore Purchase."

Q: Is there a subscription?

No. PureBeat will never have a subscription. The Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase.
