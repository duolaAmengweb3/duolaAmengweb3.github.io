Privacy Policy

Metronome: PureBeat — Last updated: May 25, 2026

Data We Collect

None. PureBeat does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device.

Analytics & Tracking

PureBeat contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking code. We do not use App Tracking Transparency because we have nothing to track.

iCloud Sync

If you use the Setlist feature (Pro), your setlist data is synced via Apple's iCloud/CloudKit. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is not accessible to us.

In-App Purchases

Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Third-Party Services

PureBeat does not integrate any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs.

Children's Privacy

PureBeat does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If we ever change this policy, we will update this page. Since we collect no data, we don't anticipate changes.
