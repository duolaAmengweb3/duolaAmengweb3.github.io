Tanwise — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Tanwise is built privacy-first. Everything stays on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, and there are no accounts and no servers.

What we collect

Nothing. Your skin profile, saved locations, sun sessions and settings are stored only on your device.

Location

Tanwise uses your location only to fetch the local UV index from Apple’s WeatherKit and to show your place name. Your location is used on-device and is never uploaded to us. You can deny or revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings.

Weather data

UV and forecast data come from Apple WeatherKit. Apple’s handling of WeatherKit requests is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Notifications

Reminders (reapply sunscreen, daily UV peak, flip) are scheduled locally on your device.

Purchases

Tanwise Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Health note

Tanwise provides general sun-safety guidance for lifestyle use. It is not medical advice and is not a medical device.
